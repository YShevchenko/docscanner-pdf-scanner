# PDF Scanner Marketing Strategy

**Document:** MARKETING.md  
**Product:** PDF Scanner (Anti-Scam Tool)  

## 1. Viability Classification
**Tier 2: Smart Utility**  
High search volume, but highly competitive. Must use comparative marketing.

## 2. Target Audience & Ad Platforms
- **Primary Platform:** Reddit Ads, Twitter, Apple Search Ads.
- **Targeting:** College students, small business owners, exact match search terms for "Adobe Scan" or "CamScanner".

## 3. Core Marketing Hook (Ad Copy)
"The only PDF Scanner that isn’t a scam. Tired of apps charging $10/month just to scan a receipt? No subscriptions. No hidden watermarks. 100% offline and private."

## 4. Geographic & Pricing Strategy
- **Tier 1:** The "anti-subscription" sentiment is strongest in the US and Europe. Focus marketing spend here at a $9.99 price point.
